Evidence tiers and independent dimensions
E0 catalog: retrievable DOI/file/condition/positive S,N; primary endpoint, loading or stress meaning may remain unresolved. Counts demonstrate collection coverage, not validated training size.
E1 candidate: database curve with explicit provenance/reuse warnings, awaiting exact source/method/figure mapping. Near-curve families belong here until resolved; similar coordinates alone neither prove duplication nor independence.
E2 source-method-supported CA: primary source supports the extracted experimental constant-amplitude observations, stress basis, endpoint/censor rules and condition. Author specimen/build IDs may be missing. Eligible for training and source-family holdout within its declared domain, with approximate digitization retained.
E3 named observations: actual author specimen IDs and direct numerical outcomes, with method/stress/censor qualifiers. Missing build IDs do not by themselves disqualify source-holdout training. Any unresolved core outcome/stress/loading issue still blocks the relevant model input; identity tier cannot repair invalid physics.
E4 physical lineage: actual specimen-to-build/production-batch mapping plus valid outcomes. Distinguish a physical build, powder lot, treatment and author campaign. Enables source-backed build analyses where enough distinct builds/campaigns exist; does not guarantee independence or coverage.
Q quarantine/relink: known variable-amplitude/equivalent lives, simulated/calculated S-N curves, unresolvable stress definitions, duplicated review curves or missing event semantics. Preserve records and reason; never treat them as new independent CA failures.

These are not a single automatic ordinal quality score. Record independent status for source access, method validity, stress semantics, censor semantics, identity, build/powder mapping, reuse, digitization and license. A fully named invalid stepped endpoint stays quarantined; an anonymous but primary-source-supported CA marker may qualify for E2 training.

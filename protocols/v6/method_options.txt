V6 METHODS AND VALIDATION OPTIONS -- PROPOSAL, NOT A FIT AUTHORIZATION
Prepared 20 October 2026. The parent's final frozen protocol, grid manifests,
and episode-design hashes supersede choices below if explicitly recorded.
No actual fatigue observations have been fitted by this proposal/audit branch.

1. Scientific question and permitted inference

Can a small number of censored constant-amplitude observations improve the
usefulness of a predicted lower-life quantile under source shift, and which
target adaptation and stress-selection choices retain acceptable empirical
risk at an identified cycle cost? The previous model-dependent high-stress
gains do not prove a physical mechanism or increase a specimen's actual life.
The useful-life metric is a change in a model's predicted q10. It is not an
R90C90 tolerance bound, a 90% population guarantee, or a calibrated confidence
limit. A physical interpretation needs independent material evidence.

All 441 current corpus outcomes, and previous external panels, have already
been inspected in earlier work. A new nested procedure separates tuning from
its own outer evaluation, but remains exploratory reuse of historical data.
Do not call the outer evaluation an unseen prospective confirmation.

The first stage establishes reasonably tuned predictors and adapter controls.
A new acquisition policy is a separate, subsequently frozen mechanism study;
neither a GP nor generic Bayesian/active stress selection is itself novel.

4. Method A: nonlinear censored Gaussian-process AFT

Y=log10(N)=m(log10(S),R)+f_global(x)+f_condition(x)+epsilon,
epsilon~Normal(0,sigma^2). The mean is a training-only censored Basquin fit;
the residual kernel is global RBF(logS,R) plus a condition random intercept
and optional condition-specific RBF(logS). Condition covariance uses working
database conditions, not asserted physical builds. For event e at y,
L=NormalPDF(y;f,sigma)^e * NormalSF(y;f,sigma)^(1-e).

Fit the joint latent posterior with Laplace's approximation at fixed kernel,
noise and mean parameters. At k>0 append ONLY selected revealed supports and
recompute the joint latent mode; do not stack a second Gaussian approximation
on an old Laplace posterior. Censored-normal factors are log-concave; use a
converged whitened Newton solve, jitter receipt and predictive-variance checks.
Predictive log-life is Gaussian under the approximation; invert this predictive
CDF for q10. Latent-function uncertainty is integrated approximately. Mean,
kernel/noise hyperparameter and model-selection uncertainty are plug-in.

Latest parent prefit proposal: globalSD {0,.3,.6}, conditionShapeSD {0,.35},
stressLength {.1,.35,1} in log10(MPa), noiseMultiplier {.5,.75,1.25,2}.
R length1, condition-shape length equal to stress length, condition-intercept
scale and reference noise computed from the current training fold. Deduplicate
identical covariance settings:72 Cartesian entries become64 when length is
irrelevant for zero global+shape kernels. Add2 explicitly specified anchors
in the final grid manifest:66 unique candidates. Do not infer their parameters
from this proposal. The emitted implementation now specifies the anchors as
zero residual covariance/noise multiplier1 and a default with globalSD .5,
shapeSD .35, stress/shape length .2 and noise multiplier1. The runner uses the
training-only hierarchical Basquin location as the shared fixed mean/reference.
The earlier24-grid suggestion is superseded BEFORE fits.

ShapeSD=0 is a condition-shape-kernel ablation, NOT an intercept-only target
update: joint conditioning can still change the uncertain global function.
The zero-global/zero-shape candidate is the strict intercept-kernel anchor.
Use a separate frozen-global approximation only if explicitly named.

A decreasing mean does not guarantee a decreasing GP q10. Log and quantify
q10 increases on a predeclared stress grid at fixed R and condition, including
extrapolation limits. Never silently isotonic-project quantiles while retaining
the old CDF/log score. A virtual-derivative monotone GP would be a different
method with an additional approximation, not an automatic property here.

5. Method B: robust heteroscedastic Student AFT, scalar target update

s=(log10(S)-trainingCenter)/trainingSD, r=R-trainingCenterR.
Let H(s,k)=(s-k)_+ -(-k)_+. Use b_j=-softplus(beta_j)<0.
Linear mean: mu=a+cR*r+b0*s.
Two-knot mean: mu=a+cR*r+b0*s+(b1-b0)H(s,k1)+(b2-b1)H(s,k2),
where k1,k2 are training-only standardized-logstress quantiles1/3 and2/3.
This is continuous with negative stress slope in each segment.
log(sigma(s))=gamma0+gamma1*s; gamma1=0 for constant scatter.
gamma0 bounds[log(.05),log(2)], gamma1 bounds[-.5,.5].
Training penalty=1e-3*(sum(b_j^2)+cR^2)+0.5*(gamma1/.25)^2;
intercept and gamma0 unpenalized; stable unconstrained raw beta.

Y=mu(x)+u_condition+sigma(s)*T_nu, u_condition~Normal(0,tau^2).
For training, maximize proper unit-factor within-condition marginal likelihoods
integrating each u; for a target condition update the SAME Normal prior by the
product of only revealed failure-density/survival factors. Nuisance population
parameters are plug-in, not a fully Bayesian hierarchical population posterior.
Use adaptive positive quadrature with explicit Gaussian-prior tail bound and
resolution checks; do not represent this as an exact analytic posterior or a
Laplace target approximation. Predict the mixture CDF and invert it; averaging
conditional/component quantiles is incorrect.

Latest parent prefit grid: nu {4,10,Normal} x scatter {constant,loglinear} x
mean {linear,two knots} x tau {.1,.3,1,3} log10-life SD =48 configurations.
The original24-grid tau {.3,1} is superseded BEFORE any real fit. tau applies
both to training condition marginals and target prior, rather than fitting
with one prior and quietly adapting with another. Two deterministic starts
are selected by the same training objective, not target validation outcomes.

This first method updates only scalar target u. Its robust tail, mean curvature
and heteroscedasticity are population flexibility, not target-learned slope or
scatter. Finite-nu Student errors on log10 life imply E[N]=infinity, so never
use an uncapped mean-life or lognormal moment shortcut. Capped expected cycles
are finite and usable. A heteroscedastic monotone location need not have
monotone q10; report the same grid diagnostic as for the GP.

6. Material physical control: two-dimensional RFL target adaptation

Retain the supported Normal-Normal RFL population variant from Pascual/Meeker,
not a claim to reproduce an author's Weibull implementation:
log10(Gamma)~Normal(muGamma+v,tauGamma^2),
Y=a+u+b*log10((S-Gamma)/Sref)+cR*(R-Rbar)+epsilon for S>Gamma,
with b<0 and N=infinity for S<=Gamma. Censor likelihood includes both finite
survival and this genuine infinite-life mass. v moves the distribution of the
random fatigue limit; u moves conditional finite log life.

Predetermined proper target prior candidate:
(u,v)~Normal(0,diag((m*tauHierTrain)^2,s_v^2)),
m in {.25,.5,1,2,4}; s_v in {.05,.15} log10-stress units:10 combinations.
Select by the same nested inner rule. The prior is an empirical-Bayes/source-
validated modeling choice, not an inferred physical-build covariance or a
population-parameter posterior. Zero correlation is explicit, not learned.
Retain the scalar-v=0 matched ablation at the SAME life-prior settings and the
local slope control, so an apparent GP gain cannot be credited merely to
granting it extra target degrees of freedom.

Integrate the 2D support posterior including fatigue-limit quadrature; verify
both domain-tail mass and resolution, and invert the integrated predictive
CDF. q10 can be infinite if finite-life mass is below0.1. Report availability
and do not replace infinity with an advantageous finite number. Include
runout-only/conflicting support, v=0 reduction, posterior normalization and
mass-at-infinity synthetic checks before any fatigue fit. No change to frozen
src/v3_rfl.py is authorized by this option.

Production predictive scoring evaluates the positive support-posterior mixture
and checks u-rule and v-rule convergence separately. Changing the u rule also
reweights v by the ratio of conditional-u marginal likelihoods, so the check
includes the u normalizer's effect on the v posterior. An omitted-posterior-mass
bound and a uniform queried-likelihood supremum give a conservative relative
numerator error estimate r; use -log(1-r) for the log-score bound, and do not
accept the fast path if r>=1. Unresolved rows fall back to
log p(query_i|supports)=log Z(supports,query_i)-log Z(supports), using separately
tail-bounded/resolution-checked positive integrals. Scoring queries affect only
evaluation of their own likelihood, never parameters, priors, acquisition or
model/support caches. Both paths evaluate the same predictive model.

The implementation uses global positive u intervals, with interval prior mass
times likelihood suprema bounding pruning; positive v nodes use one-factor and
analytic two-failure Gaussian marginal bounds. Adaptive8/16-point v intervals
and paired u rules diagnose discretization error. These rule differences are
numerical convergence estimates, not analytic error theorems. Mathematical
omitted-mass bounds are conditional on the fixed positive96-node population
Gamma quadrature representation; no rigorous continuous-Gamma quadrature-error
guarantee is claimed. This numerical statement is separate from physical or
population reliability uncertainty.

7. Outer comparison, cost and censoring

Use the SAME frozen query and selected support sequences for all predictors,
prior settings and tuned/frozen references. Four inherited policies are random,
stress-spanning, high-stress, and expected-information. The inherited planner
is the outer-training-only hierarchical/censored-Normal Fisher planner; it is
not retuned independently for GP, Student or RFL. It uses pool/query covariates
and already revealed responses only. Population-trained planner parameters
must exclude the full outer source family. Do not use outer family outcomes
to tune a policy or choose its support sequence. Predeclare all policy seeds.

Primary support cap C=10^6 makes min(N,C) identifiable for every retained
training censor endpoint. Revealed failure above C becomes a censor at C;
an earlier censor remains a censor, never invented survival to C. Query
endpoints are original. Cost is observed capped support cycles, not an
estimated future experiment time. C=10^7 is a sensitivity with lower/upper
cost identification bounds where original censor endpoints fall below C.
Include all selected rows and equal-n supports even when policies exhaust the
same pool; label such episodes uninformative for stress-policy discrimination.

For q10=q, the event of interest is Y<log10(q). An observed failure identifies
the indicator; a right censor c identifies zero if c>=log10(q), otherwise it
is unresolved. Mean risk lower/upper endpoints are identification bounds,
NOT confidence limits. For paired qB-vs-qA risk, compute inf/sup of
I(Y<qB)-I(Y<qA) over the SAME admissible Y for each censored row; subtracting
unpaired marginal extrema can be unnecessarily wide. Bootstrap source family
blocks for sampling uncertainty, never the ten seeds as independent campaigns.
Use within-row paired log-q gains and paired costs before family aggregation.
Always report both lower/upper risk and finite-quantile coverage/availability.

Suggested scientific acceptance criteria (parent must freeze exact constants):
improve equal-family held-family censored log score beyond tuned strong
references, demonstrate useful finite q10/capped-cost tradeoffs at matched k,
and do not declare success if paired risk upper confidence/identification
criteria violate a predeclared noninferiority tolerance. A +2percentage-point
risk tolerance is a proposal, not yet an authorized result-dependent threshold.
If identification is too wide to establish safety, say indeterminate; do not
turn NaN one-family CIs into failed Boolean gates. No single winning model or
condition can establish model-robust contribution. Display discordant results.

8. Later mechanism study, separate from the predictor benchmark

A possible mechanism is deciding when extra target slope/scatter/limit
adaptation is worth cycle cost under prior conflict. Compare fixed scalar,
slope-enabled and limit-enabled posterior updates, then optional model/update-
structure averaging with support marginal likelihoods. Generic model averaging,
model discrimination and quantile design are already prior art. A contribution
would require a precise new rule and evidence beyond their existing scope.

For a candidate stress x and cap C, the prospective observation is
O_C=(min(N,C),I[N<=C]), with failure density below C and a runout atom at C.
Expected capped cycles=integral from0toC of P(N>t|D,x)dt, including RFL infinite
life mass. A candidate decision rule may maximize expected reduction in
query-grid lower-quantile decision loss or posterior uncertainty, divided by
setup+expected capped cycles, with optional model-index information. Integrate
over prospective failures AND runouts before revelation. Select only existing
unrevealed candidates; do not simulate an outcome and present it as real data.
Any utility weights, update-structure priors or risk constraints require an
inner-only grid and a newly frozen comparison. This is NOT yet implemented.

Tail posterior contraction can be confidently wrong under prior/model conflict;
uncertainty reduction is not calibration. Test known synthetic misspecification
as numerical/mechanism checks, separately from experimental source evidence.
An external identity-qualified campaign remains valuable for physical-build
generalization and prospective confirmation; ordinary source-held validation
must not silently substitute for that claim.

9. Prior-art boundaries and primary sources

See literature/v6_methods/prior_art_matrix.json for evidence depth/access.
Ryan2003 DOI10.1198/1061860032012 already studies information-gain design for
the random-fatigue-limit model. Magazzeni et al. DOI10.1111/ffe.13859 applies
Bayesian entropy collection to constant-life fatigue strength. Frie et al.2023
DOI10.1111/ffe.14156 treats Bayesian optimal S-N testing with runouts. Lee et
al.2018 DOI10.1080/00401706.2018.1437475 targets lifetime-quantile precision in
sequential ALT. Wang/Li2025 DOI10.12011/SETP2023-2570 explicitly addresses
model uncertainty, prediction variance+bias and Bayesian-averaged low-life
quantiles; its full official15-page paper is now retained, with Weibull/lognormal
candidate models and Type-I right-censor likelihood verified in sections2-3.
The current2026 DOI10.1016/j.probengmech.2026.104043 presents
posterior-uncertainty/heteroscedastic stress planning and empirical within-
stress observation reservoirs (primary indexed methods; full PDF not retained).
These prevent generic active/transfer/model-discrimination novelty claims.

GP survival is established: Barrett/Coolen arXiv1312.1591. Monotone-GP
derivative information is established: Riihimaki/Vehtari PMLR9(2010)645-652.
Rindt et al. PMLR151(2022) discuss proper censored scoring. Conformal survival
methods (Candes/Lei/Ren2023 DOI10.1093/jrsssb/qkac004 and later general-right-
censor work at ICLR2025) require their stated exchangeability/censoring and
calibration conditions. They do not confer R90C90 or arbitrary new-source
coverage on this benchmark. Wu et al. DOI10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2026.109943 is
currently preview-only in our audit: do not assert its full method omits any
particular adaptive/censoring feature. Group transfer and physics baselines
alone are not a new contribution. No novelty guarantee is warranted.

Require numerical receipts for convergence, objective/start selection, grid
coverage, posterior/quadrature tails and resolution, CDF(q10)=.1, fallback,
parameter-bound hits, monotonicity violations and finite quantile availability.
Preserve failed candidates. A fresh empty-cache replay and independent paired
metric arithmetic audit precede scientific delivery. No broad model search
extension after outer inspection without an explicit new exploratory phase.

revise the research question; do not rename the old model dependence as a
physical law or claim a novel experiment policy from it.

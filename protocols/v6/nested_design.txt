Prepared before the first phase6 fit to actual fatigue observations.

Scope and status
This is exploratory reuse of an existing public-data reconstruction:441rows,

censors. Previous phases inspected these outcomes. Nested separation here is
an honest evaluation procedure within reused data, not an untouched prospective
confirmation. Source families are not certified physically independent builds,

transfer novelty is claimed. No correspondence, upload or publication.

Nested evaluation
Use12outer leave-one-source-family-out folds. For each outer family f, inner
leave-one-family-out validation uses11other eligible families g. Inner training
excludes BOTH f and g, including all linked rows. All transformations, means,
noise/prior references, knots and model population fits use that training set.
Cache populations by the66unordered pairs, not by a leaky full-data fit. The
outer final model is refitted excluding f only AFTER all inner selections are
committed. Inner plans use k0/k3 RANDOM supports and C=1e6,3seeds per condition.
The same inner query/support episodes evaluate every candidate.

Tune by censored predictive log score: failure density of log10(N), or survival
at a right-censor endpoint. Higher is better. First average query rows in an
episode, then k0/k3 equally, then seeds equally, then conditions equally within
each validation family, then the11families equally. Censoring is treated as
exogenous conditional on retained covariates/protocol, not proven universally.
A candidate needs finite scores for EVERY expected episode in that outer
fold; no dropping difficult supports or averaging only successful families.
Ties within1e-6 use frozen grid ordinal. No outer utility, risk, cost or q10 is
used to choose model hyperparameters, numerical fallbacks or acquired supports.
Preserve all candidate failures and explicit per-fold unavailable model classes.

Numerical rules and runtime
Freeze.json hashes input, episodes, exact symbolic grid and every runtime model,
selection and runner source before first actual fit. Synthetic suites and
independent mathematical reviews precede it. Cache keys include full training
observations/IDs/exclusions and freeze hash. No old unbound pickle is reused.
Retain every start/bound/fallback/numeric receipt. Deterministic retries only:
same objective/starts, larger optimizer iterations (physical1500->6000,RFL
500->2000,Student600->2400,GP100->400). No alternative model substitution.
Failed population configs are cached as unavailable. Target integration raises
on exhausted predetermined grids; unavailable outer episodes do not trigger a
different selected model. Report rather than silently drop them.
Check finite q10 using CDF(q10) and preserve failures; discrepancy>1e-3 fails
that episode numerically. Check new posterior normalization/tails, finite versus
infinite quantile classification, GP jitter/Laplace convergence and monotonicity
diagnostics. Scientific quantities use log10 life. Do not reward infinity as
finite utility. Runtime timing on synthetic data is feasibility only. The full
inner grid requires7524new GP/Student population fits plus shared physical
fits and joint-adapter scoring; hours of local computation are allowed. Keep
process handles and resumable completed shards; no result-driven grid pruning.

Analysis and evidence thresholds
Report availability, finite-q availability, q10, proper censored logscore,
risk lower/upper identification endpoints, and support-cost lower/upper bounds.
For risk I(Y<q), observed failures identify it; a censorc identifieszero when
c>=q and otherwise leaves[0,1]. Missing prediction gets risk[0,1]. For paired
qB,qA use the SAME admissible Y, not subtraction of marginal interval extrema:
failure difference is exact; censor difference lower=-I(qA>qB and qA>c),
upper=I(qB>qA and qB>c). Missing pair gives[-1,1]. Finite paired logutility is
log10(qB)-log10(qA); report its coverage alongside every number.

Average rows, seeds and conditions within source before averaging source
families. Descriptive95% intervals bootstrap source-family averages with2000
draws, RNG231. Never bootstrap seeds as independent campaigns. If fewer than2
finite source means or any required strict score/utility family is missing,
the relevant claim is indeterminate, not false or supported. These intervals
do not prove laboratory/build independence or prospective population coverage.

At k3 primary cap compare policies against random, and few-shot against k0 on
exact matched queries. Compare each predictor against inner-selected strongest
control on matched policies/k. Cost savings bounds derive from paired selected
test cost intervals, not from nominal cap times number of tests. Couple shared
selected row identities: the same unknown censored capped cost cancels in a
difference and enters both sides of a ratio. Do not invent extra uncertainty
by treating an identical selected row's cost as two unrelated variables. A useful
relative policy claim requires finite paired availability>=.95, lower95% bound
of mean logutility>=log10(1.05), upper95% paired risk-change bound<=.02, and
upper95% cost-ratio bound<=.90. Also report absolute risk upper95%<=.10 as a
separate empirical reliability criterion; never label it R90C90. A model-robust
policy contribution additionally needs the relative criterion in at least3of
the5distinct adaptive classes (hierarchical/curved,local-slope,RFL,Student,GP),
without a statistically supported utility reversal in another fully available
class. Joint/scalar RFL are one class; hierarchical/curved one class. These
are declared research decision criteria, not established physical standards.
Report all failures/discordance; thresholds do not guarantee a novel contribution.

Delivery requires all planned runs, independent paired arithmetic/source/
selection audits, fresh empty-cache replay of representative actual fits and
scientific outputs, explicit coverage/limits and source attribution. A new
acquisition rule or calibration method requires a separately frozen follow-on
study; generic Bayesian design/model averaging already has prior art. Identity-
qualified additional campaigns remain a separate objective and may not be
replaced by this source-family benchmark.

PROSPECTIVE INDEPENDENT CAMPAIGN PROPOSAL — NOT EXECUTED OR PUBLICLY REGISTERED
Prepared after all current exploratory outcomes. No new physical test is claimed.

Question: on a genuinely new specimen/build-traceable AM316L production program,
can a fixed stress-selection policy improve useful lower-life proposals over random
selection without an unacceptable increase in the censoring-identified violation rate?

Admission: use source-backed author specimen IDs and build/powder inventory; log
nominal maximum/amplitude/range convention, R, axial force control, environment,
actual temperature/frequency, surface/heat treatment and first-exposure history.
Do not mix local Kt stress or stepped outcomes with nominal virgin CA lifetimes.
Every eligible tested specimen must appear in the inventory, including runouts.
Separate physical build, powder lot, treatment and author program. Exclude any
linked historical source family from training before seeing the new outcomes.

Keep the existing six models, four policies and k0/3/5 protocol. Use k10 only when
candidate pool size permits. Hold population hyperparameters/planner fixed from
historical training; perform no outcome-guided tuning on the new program. Preserve
identical query locations and candidate pools across policies. Expose only stress/R
covariates before selection; reveal N/event only for each selected support. The
information planner is the existing censored-normal/hierarchical planner, not an
RFL-specific optimized acquisition. Query outcomes remain sealed during selection.

For actual physical validation, randomly allocate separately manufactured replicate
specimens to query and prospective policy arms. Pure replay on a complete historic
cohort reuses specimens across policy arms and is exploratory; repeated split seeds
are not new physical tests. A feasible inventory/sample-size calculation needs the
lab's available specimen budget and target violation resolution; no arbitrary power
claim follows from the present31-specimen/one-program result.

Stopping horizon and censor protocol must be set by the laboratory BEFORE outcome
collection. Match planning cap to actual censoring. If useful lower-life proposals
extend beyond1e6, include actual CA follow-up beyond that horizon where feasible;
do not infer virgin low-stress survival from later high-stress fracture. Frequency,
wall-clock capacity and budget must be recorded, not inferred from cycle counts.

Primary decision criteria remain those of frozen_design.txt: paired finite log-life
utility, availability, censor-identified risk change and absolute risk bounds, plus
observed support cycles. A higher q10 alone is not success. Report unidentified
censored comparisons, unavailable/infinite bounds and all-runout support. Aggregate
condition→author-program; uncertainty needs truly independent programs, not renamed
builds/treatments/seeds. A second program improves external evidence but does not
by itself certify90% coverage or R90C90 reliability. Report sampling uncertainty
and model-parameter uncertainty separately from censoring identification.

No stress MPa value is a universal recommendation. Within observed Merot pools,
325MPa reference and175–275MPa pre-corroded high-stress selections were explored;
use only a source/lab-supported stress grid for a new material/build and prespecify
it. No high-stress or active policy has earned general superiority in current data.

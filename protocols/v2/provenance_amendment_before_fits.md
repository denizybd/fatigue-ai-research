# Source-method gate before phase-2 fitting

2026-10-20. No phase-2 real-data fit has been run. New primary-source evidence changes the cohort before fitting.

Merot et al., DOI 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2021.106552, reports step-by-step tests with 10^6 cycles per stress step and 25 MPa stress increments. Its database curves contain 98 plotted endpoints, whereas the source reports 63 physical specimens. Without individual load histories these cannot be treated as 98 independent constant-amplitude lifetimes. Exclude this DOI from every valid phase-2 fit/calibration/test, including the full-family sensitivity. Quarantine the linked modelling continuation DOI 10.1016/j.engfracmech.2022.108883 until constant-amplitude specimen provenance is resolved.

This is a correction to the v1 data-eligibility assumption. V1's exact computational replay still holds, but its empirical results are conditional on a now-known imperfect cohort. They cannot be promoted as a validated constant-amplitude fatigue benchmark. The old results stay immutable for audit; a correction addendum accompanies any future communication package.

Three Blinn papers (DOIs 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2019.03.022, 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2019.105301 and 10.1051/matecconf/201816502006) contain physical constant-amplitude tests alongside calculated PhyBaL curves and load-increase tests. Their titles alone do not warrant rejection. Until plotted database points are tied to the physical-test symbols, quarantine these sources from the strict primary cohort rather than assume every extracted point is a measured lifetime.

Main analysis will use a source-method whitelist frozen in a machine-readable evidence gate. Other unverified sources can enter only explicitly labelled broader-data sensitivities. Do not downgrade a failed fit or performance after seeing its outcomes by changing this gate. Review abstracts versus full methods versus figure-level correspondence separately.

The newly found external campaign is Trombley et al. (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-97031-1, repository DOI 10.13011/m3-c93d-z035. Public specimen pages provide 129 complete as-built records in three diameter groups (44, 40, 45), out of the 130 stated in the article. One page lacks a required endpoint/stress and is excluded without imputation. The external endpoint is an ultrasonic resonance-drop/visible-crack criterion, not full fracture. Its three groups constitute one related experimental campaign. Some tests predate the article; new publication year is not the independence argument. Dataset license is unspecified; local records remain out of the shareable archive until clarified.

Freeze external support/query IDs before fitting, k=0,3,5,10 and the same held-query rows for all k. Treat historical random row selection as retrospective endpoint availability, not actual first-in-time experimentation or active design. Report author-provided test dates and the source staircase protocol as limitations. No hypothetical censoring time is assigned to failed specimens. Compare raw and source-calibrated model bounds; no R90C90 claim. The paper's Weibull random-fatigue-limit estimator is not recreated by the present Basquin working model, so external results cannot establish superiority over that physical baseline.

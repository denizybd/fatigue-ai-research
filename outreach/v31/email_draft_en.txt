Your work on probabilistic fatigue prediction and transfer learning motivated a public-data study of which few stress tests help when moving to a new production condition.

We recovered traceable virgin first loading blocks from Mérot's stepped-load campaign and compared four selection policies with six censored models on identical queries. The acquisition subset is 31 specimens from two builds within one author campaign. We therefore make no general reliability or policy-superiority claim and do not claim to reproduce or outperform your original BNN.

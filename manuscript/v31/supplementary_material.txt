S4. Prediction model formulas and limitations
All logs are base10. For a lognormal AFT model with conditional mean mu, scatter sigma, observed y and event e, each contribution is f(y|mu,sigma)^e × SF(y|mu,sigma)^(1−e), including the relevant target-effect integration. A runout contributes survival, not a density at a fictional failure time.

The RFL uses log10 Gamma~Normal(mu_Gamma,tau_Gamma²). If Gamma<S, log10 N~Normal(a+b log10((S−Gamma)/Sref)+c(R−Rbar)+u,sigma²), b<0; otherwise N=infinity. Finite-failure mass p(S)=Phi((log10 S−mu_Gamma)/tau_Gamma). The marginal finite CDF integrates the conditional CDF only over Gamma<S; the survivor includes its complement and the infinity atom. A predictive alpha quantile is infinite if alpha>=p(S); otherwise it solves the marginal CDF at alpha. The target shift u has a proper normal prior SD fixed from training hierarchical tau and is integrated using revealed support outcomes. It changes finite-life location, not the fatigue-limit distribution. This is not a hierarchical RFL population posterior or a reproduction of Trombley's Weibull model.

Source-balanced working likelihood, fixed penalties/bounds/starts and load-ratio terms are implementation choices. Population parameter uncertainty is omitted. Low-data identifiability is not guaranteed by numerical convergence. Selected fit diagnostics, all starts and fallback status are retained. The two extension population RFL fits converge without selected bound hits or fallback; earlier historical leave-source fits can hit bounds and remain flagged.

S5. Acquisition information and allowed query knowledge
Fixed hierarchical/censored-normal Fisher planning produces ONE selected-support sequence per policy/condition/seed. ALL SIX models receive that sequence; the planner is not independently retuned or optimized for RFL. Known query covariates (stress and R) are allowed as a transductive fixed prediction grid. Query outcomes are never exposed. Candidate unknown endpoints/events are never used before selection.

For z=(log10 C−mu)/sigma, expected location information is I=[Phi(z)−z phi(z)+phi(z)^2/Phi(−z)]/sigma². With current intercept/slope covariance V, candidate x=(1,logS−center), and Q=mean(x_query x_query'), score=I×(x'VQVx)/(1+I×x'Vx). Prior center/scatter/intercept SD are training-derived; slope SD=max(1,|training slope|/2), fixed in advance. Current MAP/Hessian use selected supports only. This local linear/Gaussian criterion is approximate and established test-design ideas are not new by implementation.

For support observation (N,e), y_C=log10 min(N,C), e_C=e×1(N<=C). Earlier author runouts remain censored at their own stop. Observed support exposure is sum min(N,C) for selected supports, not the expected duration of invented future tests. It excludes manufacture, machine time, frequency conversion and setup cost.

S6. Risk, pairing, unavailable values and aggregation
Absolute empirical risk is partially identified by r_low=mean[e×1(L>y)] and r_high=mean[1(L>y)]. For an unavailable L, use0 and1 respectively. Positive infinity is available, but unsafe for any observed finite failure. Report ordinary availability, finite availability and infinity rate separately.

For two available bounds A and B on the same failure, paired difference d=1(A>y)−1(B>y) is exact. On the same right-censor, its lower limit is −1(B>A and B>y), and upper limit is1(A>B and A>y). If either prediction is unavailable, the limits are−1 and+1. These ranges exploit the shared unknown failure time; they are not sampling confidence intervals.

Finite-pair utility is mean(A−B) on identical queries where both are finite; 10^utility is a geometric life ratio. It is not demonstrated safe-life improvement. Queries average within condition/seed, conditions within campaign/seed, then seeds within campaign; campaigns receive equal weight. Bootstrap2,000/seed231 applies at campaign level only when at least two campaigns exist. With one campaign lo95/hi95 remain missing, and success gates are NOT EVALUABLE. Do not convert missing-interval false flags into substantive failure counts.

S7. Result CSV meanings
predictions.csv: one row per query specimen, seed, policy, feasible k, model and cap. row_id/dataset_id/campaign_id/panel/identity_gate identify scientific units and gate. log_life,event are unchanged query endpoint/censor. log_stress/load_ratio are known query covariates. log_lower is proposed q10 in log cycles, possibly missing or +infinity. n_support_failure and support_cycles describe revealed support after truncation. fit_signature is computational cache metadata, not a scientific measurement. Repeated rows are not independent specimens.

selections.csv: one row per selected candidate and acquisition rank. Its k is the sequential selection rank (1,2,...), so prefixes k<=3 or k<=5 define budgets. It is not a separate repeated experiment for each final budget. stress_amplitude is selected recorded MPa; revealed_event/revealed_log_life enter only after selection. selection_score records the design score or policy ordering. cap is the planning/truncation setting.

summary.csv: equal condition/campaign/seed aggregate mean by panel/policy/model/k/cap/metric; n_campaigns counts author campaigns; lo95/hi95 are campaign bootstrap intervals when available. n_finite_campaigns counts finite metric means.

paired_comparisons.csv: same-query comparisons versus_random or versus_k0. Metrics include paired/finite availability, finite log_life_gain, paired true_risk_change_lo/hi and support_cycle_change. Means are not independent query-row averages across campaigns.

by_condition.csv: intermediate condition/campaign/seed means for heterogeneity inspection. success_gates.csv: numerical gate ingredients and booleans; missing campaign intervals mean non-evaluable. query_identity_checks.json: explicit common-query equality. run_receipt.json: data/protocol/code hashes, splits, training source sets and population fit diagnostics.

S11. Zero-support reference for the matched-intercept label
At k=0 the runner explicitly substitutes the hierarchical population prediction as a common starting reference. The target-only unpooled intercept is estimated only after supports are observed; for k>0 all-censored supports leave it unidentified. Therefore its versus_k0 contrast uses the hierarchical reference, rather than an otherwise undefined no-support local intercept. Main k3-versus-random tables are unaffected.

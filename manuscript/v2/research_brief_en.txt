Few-shot fatigue limits after a provenance audit
AM316L | Working technical brief | Computational benchmark, not a validated safe-design method
Question
Can a few endpoints from a new AM316L condition make lower-life limits both safer and useful? We tested
censored monotone Basquin baselines, target-intercept updating and hierarchical borrowing, using fixed
support/query splits and held-out source blocks.
Concrete evidence
Original-methods review reduced a 1,126-point public pilot to 309 points/32 curves/11 method-reviewed
source blocks for the main cohort. It identified stepped-load observations, republished curves and
source-supported runout/treatment corrections. Physical specimen/batch independence remains
incompletely documented. These are corrections to this open-data benchmark, not proof of errors in
Centola et al.’s original 518 records.
Results
In the secondary full-training raw track, three endpoints per condition increased geometric lower life 1.92x
and reduced mean danger identification endpoints from 13.35-15.41% to 8.31-8.93%. The descriptive 95%
source-bootstrap life-gain interval is 0.84-3.65x; risk worsens for several sources. The prespecified 90%
calibration becomes a zero-life bound with the available source count, so its usefulness criterion fails.
An acquired author-provided campaign contains 129 complete specimens in three diameter groups,
representing one related campaign. Three random endpoints per group (nine total) reduce danger from
53.66% to 13.19% by lowering overly optimistic limits. A matched simple target-intercept control gives
6.99% at shorter life. No hierarchical superiority or general 90% source-conditional guarantee is
established. The campaign uses a crack-related ultrasonic endpoint, not necessarily full fracture.
A narrow next step
A useful continuation would test stress-level selection at fixed query sets and matched experimental
budgets against a random-fatigue-limit baseline and simple local updating. This needs specimen/build
lineage, consistent failure definitions, actual censoring plans and additional held-out campaigns. A
source-to-condition mapping or brief methodological feedback would resolve a concrete bottleneck.

The dataset contains data from mechanical tests  (LCF tests, the 1st quarter-cycle, and calculation of alternative loop shape parameter),  datasets on statistical treatment of microstructural features evaluated from SEM and TEM micrographs, and calculations of softening ratios related to the publication: "On the origin of cyclic softening in fatigued 316L austenitic stainless steel with unique cellular solidification/dislocation substructure manufactured additively by selective laser melting”. The data were collected between 1/2022 and 6/2025.

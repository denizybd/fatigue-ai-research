Net shape specimens in the as-built state were fatigued at room temperature under a fully reversed loading cycle (Rε = −1) with three constant total strain amplitudes of 0.4%, 0.5%, and 0.8%, and a constant strain rate of 5 × 10−3 s−1 up to the end of fatigue life. During cyclic straining, hysteresis loops were recorded with a resolution of 800 points per loop for their subsequent analysis.

Data contains the stress amplitude, plastic strain amplitude, and calculated Vh parameters for each tested specimen.

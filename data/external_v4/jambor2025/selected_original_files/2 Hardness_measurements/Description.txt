The dataset contains hardness data used for Figure 3. The hardness was measured using a universal nanomechanical testing machine, Zwick ZHN, equipped with a Vickers indenter by imposing a force of 150 mN for 30 seconds. The dataset contains results obtained for 4 measured lines, 2 on the longitudinal sample and 2 on the transversal sample. The obtained values are stored in a CSV file, in the form of distance from the surface (in micrometers) and measured hardness (in GPa).

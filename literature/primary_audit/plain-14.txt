Fig. 9 d shows that with small values of h a better fatigue response can be achieved. This behavior is particularly evident for the 350
MPa stress level. At 200 MPa there is no marked difference between values of h ranging from 60 to 90 μ m, whereas a more prominent

but it can be observed that low values of t generally give a better fatigue response. It is generally known that t has a lower influence on
the life [11,12,17,18] and Section 4.2.2 analyzes this peculiarity. Nonetheless, [23] confirms the trends found by the BNN, since low

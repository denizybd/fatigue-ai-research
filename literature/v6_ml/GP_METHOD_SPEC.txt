Established censored GP-AFT comparison baseline -- phase6

Y=log10(N), x=(log10(stress amplitude in MPa), load ratio R).
The fixed mean is the raw-coefficient mean of the hierarchical censored
Basquin model fitted only to the current training fold. That legacy mean
estimator uses a source/condition-balanced composite marginal objective.
Its within-condition sigma and condition-intercept tau provide training-only
reference scales. The GP residual likelihood itself has unit row factors;
this estimator difference is explicit in the parent's frozen protocol.

The residual covariance sums a global squared-exponential kernel over stress
and R, a working-condition intercept kernel, and an optional working-condition
squared-exponential stress kernel. Working conditions do not certify physical
builds or independent experiments. An observed failure contributes Normal
density in log10 life; a right censor contributes Normal survival at its stop.

At fixed mean, noise and covariance parameters, a whitened Newton solver finds
the joint latent mode with log-concave factors. Laplace covariance integrates
latent uncertainty approximately. Selected horizon-capped observations are
appended to the ORIGINAL training likelihood and the joint mode is recomputed;
the old posterior mode is only a warm start. Mean/kernel/noise parameters stay
fixed. There is no repeated Laplace approximation of an old Gaussian posterior.
Predictive loglife is Normal under the approximation; q10 is its 0.1 quantile.

Grid details are generated by proposed_gp_grid; the parent grid manifest and
protocol, once frozen, govern the actual experiment. Proposed core global SD
{0,.3,.6}, condition-shape SD{0,.35}, stress length{.1,.35,1.0}, and noise
multiplier{.5,.75,1.25,2.0} has64 unique covariance/noise settings after inert
length removal. Two anchors make66. R length1, condition-shape length equals
stress length, condition-intercept SD=tau_train except the zero-covariance
anchor. Noise=noise_multiplier*sigma_train. All hyperparameter selection is
inner source-family predictive censored logscore only, never outer q10/risk.

No mean/kernel/noise hyperparameter uncertainty is integrated. No infinite-
life atom is included. Monotonicity of q10 is not guaranteed and is diagnosed,
never silently projected. A zero condition-shape kernel does not eliminate
all shape updating when the uncertain global kernel remains. q10 is not an
R90C90 tolerance bound, and generated-data checks are not physical validation.
GP-survival/AFT and Laplace inference have prior art; no GP novelty is claimed.

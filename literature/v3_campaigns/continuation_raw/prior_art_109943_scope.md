# Wu et al., DOI 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2026.109943: bounded scope audit

Verified bibliography: International Journal of Fatigue, article 109943, January 2027 issue. Crossref record created 22 August 2026; that date is not a verified publication-online date. Authors: Zicheng Wu, Yi Zhou, Ninian Sing Kok Ho, John Hock Lye Pang.

Primary access is limited to indexed publisher previews. A web search of the exact full title on 20 October 2026 returned the [ScienceDirect article preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0142112326004640) and [section snippets](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0142112326004640). Direct page fetching returned 403; public Elsevier API returned bibliographic coredata, not body text. No full paper or supplement was obtained. OpenAlex and Eureka are not the evidence for the method statements below.

The indexed article preview describes 12,518 FatigueData-AM2022 failure points across 96 alloys, four ML models and three physics-baseline tiers. It compares random points, groups and leave-one-material-family-out evaluations; the latter is not established as a physical-build holdout. The abstract reports group/random XGBoost R² 0.51/0.69 and recovery through roughly 60 target points. The exact group key, target point selection, acquisition policy, specimen/build identifiers, censoring likelihood and safe-life coverage remain unverified. Therefore these details cannot support a claim that stress-adaptive few-test reliability bounds are absent.

The indexed section preview names XGBoost, LightGBM and Random Forest. It states that code/configurations/seeds and in-house measurements require author requests; external digitized series come from references 27–29. Those references and supplementary tables were not obtained.

The source establishes a serious overlap warning for broad claims about leakage control, physics baselines and target augmentation. Full-text comparison is still required before a defensible novelty statement.

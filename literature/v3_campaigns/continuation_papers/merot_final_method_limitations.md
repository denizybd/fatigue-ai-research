# Method limitations and endpoint interpretation

1. **Endpoint is unchanged.** Raw `Fracture=oui` is the authors' rupture endpoint. Every retained initial-block failure has no later raw loading block. Thesis PDF114–115 describes force-controlled uniaxial fatigue and running samples to rupture. The first block is physically constant amplitude; the later stepped history is not used. This recovery does not create a different failure criterion.

2. **Censoring is by design.** Most initial blocks stop at1e6cycles; some salt-spray blocks stop at1e7. `Fracture=non` means survival to that recorded stop, not an observed fatigue lifetime. Never score these censor times as exact failures or infer their lifetime at the initial stress from a later, higher-stress failure. Data above the fixed censor horizon are unobserved; full tail coverage/useful upper life limits cannot be directly verified from these censored queries alone.

3. **Treatments precede fatigue.** Machining/polishing, pre-corrosion, EDM defects and small-volume geometry are prepared before cyclic fatigue, as documented on PDF110–115. They are condition descriptors. Fractography, critical defect measurements and equivalent-life corrections are postfailure and have been removed from final verified data.

4. **F8is a documented pre-corrosion exception.** F8=PM-04-F14, physical buildFS19-006, powderTLS1051/1/1. It was exposed to salt spray for85days before an air fatigue test; most other `Corrodé air` specimens were electrochemically pre-corroded. Its first block is275MPa amplitude,20°C,15Hz, survived1e6cycles. It is included in the frozen43nominal/52air data, within a build×treatment condition of only2records. Keep the frozen primary unchanged and report an exclude-F8sensitivity for pooled diagnostics. A policy requiring at least6records per acquisition condition already omits its small condition. F7, the other80day salt-spray exception, is excluded for physical-key collision.

5. **Stress basis matters.** Standard geometry uses nominal macroscopic gauge stress; source tables show σnom=σmax. Nine small-volume records use local peak gauge stress σmax=Kt·σnom with specimen-specific pretest geometry. Exact Ktvalues were not verified; do not derive an exact conversion from rounded final-stage stress columns. Their use is separate exploratory local-stress evidence, with an exclude-small-volume sensitivity for any pooled transfer analysis.

6. **Identity conflicts remain excluded.** Ten physical coordinate keys collide across21author aliases;12aliases have incomplete raw coordinates, and K2has multipleStep0rows. Some collisions recur in the thesis; C9has an explicit raw/thesis plate contradiction. No author-version preference, coordinate correction or missing metadata was silently imputed. Seventy retained records have unique raw physical keys and exact matching thesis full references.

7. **Independence is bounded.** Three production builds are not three independent publications. Merot106552, Merot108883 and reused comparisons in Auffray109107 belong to one source family and must be excluded from population training. This is retrospective source recovery after historical outcomes were available, not fresh outcome-blind confirmation.
